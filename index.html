High-Grade Serous Ovarian Carcinoma: Evidence by Treatment Setting

Scope is high-grade serous carcinoma only. Low-grade serous is a different disease and is not handled here. Ovarian trials are 70–97% HGSC, so the trial-wide HR is close to an HGSC HR; what it hides is the biomarker: BRCA1/2, HRD without BRCA, HR-proficiency, FRα, PD-L1. Read the biomarker line before the ITT HR, especially for any PARP inhibitor row.
