OVCA Decision Engine

The ranker takes a patient profile and orders regimens by overall-survival evidence first, then by the adverse-effect profile against the patient's own risks and prior-therapy ledger. High-grade serous carcinoma carries the OS evidence; for clear cell, endometrioid and mucinous carcinoma the engine ranks on histotype-level evidence and says so when it is extrapolating. The evidence browser holds the trial rows and references of all four vault notes. Every number traces to a vault extract, a PubMed record or a DailyMed label.
